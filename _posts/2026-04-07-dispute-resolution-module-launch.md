We're excited to announce the **Dispute Resolution** module — a starter kit for managing internal complaints, grievances, and disputes with structured investigation, mediation, and appeals processes.

## What is the Dispute Resolution Module?

The Dispute Resolution module provides structured data capture for managing dispute cases, investigations, mediations, determinations, and appeals. It enables organizations to maintain fair and consistent dispute handling processes, document investigations with evidence and interview tracking, facilitate alternative dispute resolution through mediation, make formal determinations with findings, track corrective actions, and process appeals with full audit trails and confidentiality controls.

Key capabilities include:

- **Intake and Case Management** – Capture initial complaints and concerns, create formal dispute cases with classification and assignment, support anonymous reporting and early resolution
- **Party Management** – Document complainants, respondents, witnesses, representatives, and decision makers with role-based access and confidentiality controls
- **Investigation Documentation** – Assign investigators, define scope and methodology, conduct interviews, collect and organize evidence, track investigation completion
- **Issue Analysis** – Structure specific allegations or claims within cases, link policy and regulatory citations, support multi-issue disputes
- **Findings and Determinations** – Record investigation conclusions (substantiated, unsubstantiated, inconclusive), make formal decisions with remedies and actions
- **Mediation Support** – Facilitate alternative dispute resolution sessions, track mediation outcomes, document settlement agreements
- **Corrective Actions** – Assign remediation requirements (training, discipline, policy updates, monitoring), track completion and effectiveness
- **Appeals Processing** – Document challenges to determinations, manage appellate review, track appeal outcomes (affirmed, reversed, modified, remanded)
- **Referral Management** – Route cases to HR, legal, compliance, or external agencies with referral tracking

## What's Included

This initial release (v1.1.0.0) provides the foundational data model:

- **13 entities** providing comprehensive dispute lifecycle management
- **Integration with Core module** for persons, organizations, documents, legal authorities, compliance frameworks, and agreements

Forms and views for case tracking, investigation documentation, mediation coordination, and determination processing will be available in an upcoming release. The module provides implementors with a baseline data structure that can be customized for employee relations departments, labor relations offices, ombudsman programs, ethics offices, equal employment opportunity offices, and organizational compliance teams.

## Getting Started

The Dispute Resolution module is available in the [FAST Modules Repository](https://github.com/microsoft/industry-apps) under the MIT license. It requires the Core module as a foundation.
