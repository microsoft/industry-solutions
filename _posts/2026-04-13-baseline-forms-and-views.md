We're pleased to announce that all industry solution modules now include baseline user interface components, making it easier to start working with module data immediately after installation.

## What's New

Every module now ships with essential forms and views:

- **Main Forms** – Each primary entity includes a comprehensive main form with organized tabs and sections, displaying key fields and related information through subgrids that show connected records
- **Active Views** – Default views are configured for browsing and filtering active records, with relevant columns pre-configured for common workflows
- **Quick Create Forms** – Streamlined quick create forms enable rapid data entry for frequently created records without leaving your current context

These baseline UI components provide a functional starting point for data entry and management across all modules. Organizations can use these forms and views as-is for immediate productivity or customize them to match specific organizational processes and requirements.

## Modules Updated

All 20 industry solution modules include these baseline forms and views:

**Workforce & HR**: HR Administration, HR Benefits, HR Recruiting, Time Travel & Expenses, Training & Certification

**Compliance & Security**: Investigations, Personnel Security, Dispute Resolution

**Operations**: Asset Management, IT Service Management, Operational Excellence, Project Tracking, Request Tracker

**Government & Legal**: Court Case Management

**Financial**: Financial Management

**External Engagement**: Event Management, Programs & Services

**Administrative**: Executive Coordination, Gamification

**Foundation**: Core

## Getting Started

Modules are available for download from the [modules page]({{ '/modules' | relative_url }}). Each module release (v1.2.0.0 for most modules, v1.3.0.0 for Court Case Management) includes these baseline forms and views ready for immediate use. As always, these are starter kits designed to provide a foundation that you can adapt and extend to meet your organization's specific needs.
